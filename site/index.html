Block the ads. Read the fine print.

Caveatly is a free Chrome extension that blocks ads and trackers on every site, turns on the privacy settings Chrome leaves off, and reads a site’s terms and privacy policy so you know what you agreed to.

An ad blocker that works on every site

Ads and trackers are stopped before they load, so pages are faster and quieter. Caveatly uses the same public filter lists as the big blockers, and nothing about your browsing leaves your computer to do it.

Ads and trackers
EasyList and EasyPrivacy rules, run by Chrome’s own blocking engine.
Video ads
Including the ones before YouTube videos.
Leftover ad boxes
Hidden, so pages close up where the ads were.
Sponsored results
Gone from Google, Amazon, Bing and DuckDuckGo, in every country.
Anything else
Right-click it and choose Block element.
A site you want to support
One click pauses blocking there.

The fine print, read for you

Open Caveatly on any site and it finds the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, reads every clause with Google Gemini, and flags what matters.

  1. 1

    Finds the legal pages

    From footer links, common addresses like /privacy, or the page you are already reading. HTML and PDF both work.

  2. 2

    Reads every clause

    The policy text goes to Gemini with your free API key. Each flag comes back in plain English with the clause and a verbatim quote.

  3. 3

    Grades the whole thing

    Flags are sorted into three tiers and the site gets a grade from A to F.

Safe

Terms that favour you, like deleting your data whenever you ask.

Caution

Worth knowing, like subscriptions that renew on their own.

Concern

Read before agreeing, like selling your data or waiving your right to sue.

Privacy guards, on by default

Caveatly switches on the protections most people never find in Chrome’s settings. Each one is a single toggle if a site needs it off.

  • Cookie banners answered. Caveatly clicks “necessary only” or “reject all”, and hides the notices it can’t answer.
  • Fewer pop-ups, if you want. Turn on Hide annoyances to drop newsletter pop-ups, share bars and chat bubbles.
  • Chrome’s ad features off. Topics, Protected Audience and ad measurement stop profiling you for advertisers.
  • Clean links. Tracking tags like utm_*, gclid and fbclid are removed, and redirect links from Google, Facebook and others go straight to where they point.
  • No IP leaks through WebRTC. Video-call features can’t reveal your local network address.
  • No click pings. Links can’t report your clicks back to a tracker.
  • Do-not-sell signal. Every site gets the Global Privacy Control signal.
  • Third-party cookies. One toggle blocks them everywhere. It’s off by default because some sign-ins rely on them.
  • Less sent to Google. An optional toggle stops Chrome sending mistyped addresses and spell-check text to Google.

What leaves your browser

Almost nothing. There is no account and no developer server in the path.

  • Ad blocking is local. The rules ship inside the extension and Chrome applies them. Caveatly never sees or logs what it blocks.
  • Only policy text goes to Gemini, and only when you scan, with a key that stays on your device.
  • One anonymous ping a day with a random install ID and version numbers. Turn it off in Settings.
  • Open source. Every line is on GitHub. The privacy policy has the details.

Install in two minutes

Prefer to build it yourself? git clone the repo, run npm install && npm run build, and load dist/.

  1. Open Caveatly on the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome. Ad blocking and the privacy guards start right away.
  2. Pin Caveatly to the toolbar.
  3. To read the fine print, get a free API key from Google AI Studio and paste it into Caveatly → Settings.
